🔢 Number Theory

The Queen of Mathematics — Gauss

Prime Numbers

Integers >1 divisible only by 1 and themselves. Infinitely many (Euclid's proof). The largest known prime (as of 2024) has over 24 million digits.

Modular Arithmetic

a ≡ b (mod n) if n divides (a-b). Foundation of cryptography (RSA).

Diophantine Equations

Polynomial equations seeking integer solutions. Example: x² + y² = z² (Pythagorean triples).

Fermat's Last Theorem: xⁿ + yⁿ = zⁿ has no integer solutions for n > 2 (proved by Wiles, 1994)

Prime Number Theorem

The number of primes ≤ x is approximately x / ln(x). π(x) ~ x/ln(x).

Goldbach Conjecture: Every even integer > 2 is sum of two primes. Still unproven.

📐 Algebra & Abstract Structures

Group Theory

A group (G,*) satisfies closure, associativity, identity, inverse. Symmetry groups classify molecular structures & particle physics.

Linear Algebra

Vectors, matrices, eigenvalues. Powers quantum mechanics, ML, and graphics.

Galois Theory

Connects field extensions to group theory. Proved impossibility of solving quintic by radicals.

Eigenvalue equation: A𝐯 = λ𝐯

Matrices represent linear transformations. Determinant = 0 means singular (non-invertible).

📈 Calculus & Analysis

Limits & Continuity

lim(x→a) f(x) = L. ε-δ definition formalized by Cauchy and Weierstrass.

Derivatives

df/dx = lim(h→0) [f(x+h)-f(x)]/h. Measures instantaneous rate of change.

Integration

Riemann integral: area under curve. Fundamental Theorem links derivative & integral.

∫ₐᵇ f(x)dx = F(b) - F(a) where F' = f

Multivariable & Vector Calculus

Gradient, divergence, curl. Stokes' Theorem: ∫∂M ω = ∫M dω.

📐 Geometry & Topology

Euclidean Geometry

5 postulates. Parallel postulate led to non-Euclidean geometries.

Differential Geometry

Curves, surfaces, manifolds. Gaussian curvature. Used in General Relativity.

Topology

Properties invariant under continuous deformation. Coffee cup = donut (genus 1).

Poincaré Conjecture: Every simply connected closed 3-manifold is homeomorphic to S³. Proved by Perelman (2003).

🎲 Probability & Statistics

Bayes' Theorem

P(A|B) = P(B|A)P(A)/P(B). Foundation of modern inference.

Distributions

Normal (Gaussian), Poisson, Binomial. Central Limit Theorem: sample means → normal.

Normal PDF: f(x) = (1/σ√2π) e^(-(x-μ)²/(2σ²))

🔢 Discrete Mathematics

Graph Theory

Vertices, edges. Eulerian paths, Hamiltonian cycles. Four Color Theorem.

Combinatorics

Counting, permutations: n!/(n-r)!. Binomial coefficients.

🌀 Complex Analysis & Chaos

Complex Numbers

z = a + bi, i² = -1. Euler: e^(iθ) = cosθ + i sinθ.

Mandelbrot Set

zₙ₊₁ = zₙ² + c. Fractal boundary exhibits infinite complexity.

e^(iπ) + 1 = 0 (Euler's Identity)
